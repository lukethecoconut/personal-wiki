## 🧐 About <a name = "about"></a>
I am wired to forget. The names of close friends, details of prominent events, and faces of the people I've met all fade away in my brain. It's always an awkward moment when having to explain this, as memories are often markers of a relationship. If I don't explain, people will view me as a person who does not place the same importance to the relationship we share as they do. Forgetfulness is an active barrier I face to a social life.

Here is my proposition: a LLM journalling buddy who's here to help you review your memories when you need them.

The principles of the project:
-	**The user ***ALWAYS*** has full control of their data**. All data stays within a server we control and run, not with a third-party company’s API with questionable data retention policies. For even fuller control, users will be given an option to download an open-source LLM model on their computer, keeping all data within their local server.
-	**A journaling aid, not a replacement**. Your thoughts and writing are always at the forefront, acting as the primary sources of the "Wiki". Therefore, to encourage reviewing one's own notes rather than the LLM's, all summaries will be minimal, with cross-references to original material being foreheaded. This program will act as a guidepost, not the destination themselves.

The features I intend to create:
- Options to choose what type of LLM model for the program. Cloud API, open-source on our servers, or a locally-run model.
- Calendar interface.
- Search interface.
- Wiki interface.
- Ability to edit the wiki directly.
- Feature to handle faulty memory.
- Feature to handle vague, informal, pronoun-heavy language (event/person extraction).
